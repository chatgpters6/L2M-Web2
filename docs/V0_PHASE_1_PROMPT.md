# v0 — First-Round Design Prompt (Stage 1 Only)

You are the lead digital product and brand design director for Legacy2Modern.

**Before producing any UI, read and follow the repository's canonical design brief:**
[docs/L2M_WEB_V0_HIGH_FIDELITY_DESIGN_BRIEF_V1.0.md](L2M_WEB_V0_HIGH_FIDELITY_DESIGN_BRIEF_V1.0.md)

The L2M website V1.2 commercial baseline is approved. Do not change product positioning, target buyers, the conversion model or the design delivery boundaries.

## This round: exactly two distinct visual directions

Only design **the public homepage Hero (Screen 01) and Flagship Website Transformation (Screen 02)**.

Create two distinct high-fidelity clickable prototypes:

### Direction A — Editorial Product
International editorial brand aesthetic, assertive typography, considered whitespace, refined layout, and premium real-website presentation. It should convey taste and credibility to business owners.

### Direction B — Interactive Product
Put a striking browser-based Before / After website transformation front and center. Realistic UI, understandable switching, clear URL input, compact proof hotspots. It should explain L2M's value before the user reads much text.

## Exact core copy

- Chinese headline: **让现有网站，成为更有说服力的客户入口。**
- English headline: **Make your existing website work better for your customers.**
- Chinese subtitle: **从现有网站出发，升级视觉设计、内容结构与客户行动路径。先了解哪里值得升级，再决定下一步。**
- Primary CTA: **分析我的网站 / Analyze My Website**
- Secondary CTA: **查看真实改造演示 / See a Transformation**
- Flagship title: **Same business. A completely different experience.**
- Brand palette direction: Deep Ink #17262D, Warm Ivory #F7F7F2, Electric Lime #B8F06B, Soft Mist #D9E5E4.

## Mandatory interactions

- URL input displayed above the fold on desktop and mobile, with realistic invalid/empty states.
- Before/After switch, default to After.
- Desktop/Mobile comparison view, and relevant page tabs only when source material exists.
- Up to three explanatory commercial hotspots; do not simulate a real received lead.
- Working high-fidelity interactive demo states, not merely static image proposals.
- Desktop at 1440px; mobile at 390px. Consider 360px narrow mobile.

## Truth and safety constraints

- If no authorized same-site before/after images are supplied, show clearly marked **DEMO PLACEHOLDER** media. Never fake a real customer case with unrelated website screenshots.
- Do not invent testimonials, companies, metrics, conversion promises, prices, successful AI analyses or paid orders.
- No live integrations, AI analysis, payments, auth, crawling, deployments or production launches.
- Don't generate Screen 03-08 or the account/product workspace yet.
- Hosting is a minor optional downstream service, not a central visual topic.

## Expected deliverables

1. Direction A: 1440px desktop + 390px mobile actual previews.
2. Direction B: 1440px desktop + 390px mobile actual previews.
3. A concise comparison of brand character, first-10-second value clarity, visual impact, and CTA visibility.
4. A list of Demo Placeholders and missing real assets.
5. Notes on responsiveness, keyboard/touch controls and reduced-motion support.

**STOP after the two candidate designs.** Do not silently merge them or continue the rest of the site. Ask for the owner's design review.
